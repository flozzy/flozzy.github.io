About Young Mothers, Inc.

Our Mission

Young Mothers, Inc. educates, mentors, and supports young mothers on their journey to becoming self-sufficient for themselves and their families. As a 501(c)(3) organization, we go beyond referrals offering hands-on assistance that helps young women build self-esteem, gain practical skills, and achieve lasting independence.

Your donation directly funds these efforts, giving young mothers the tools and support they need to create a stable, hopeful future for themselves and their children.

Our Impact

For over 13 years, we've helped more than 1,000 young mothers and their children build a path toward self-sufficiency with the support of over 2,000 volunteers across the DMV region.

Why You Can Trust Your Gift Matters

Young Mothers, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) federal tax-exempt organization. Every donation is tax-deductible, and you'll receive a receipt for your records.

We fundraise through Zeffy, ensuring 100% of your donation goes directly to our mission with zero platform fees taken out.

We're committed to transparency in how your support creates real change for young mothers and their families.
